Does this work on ES or other instruments?

The system is built and optimized for MNQ. The concepts apply to ES and NQ, but the specific levels, sizing logic, and examples are MNQ-specific. Use it on other instruments at your own discretion.

How much time does this require per day?

15–20 minutes of pre-market prep before 9:30 ET, then active screen time from 9:30 to approximately 11:00 ET when most setups form. The guide includes a no-trade rule after 11:30 ET.

MNQ requires roughly $500–$1,000 in margin per contract depending on your broker. The guide covers position sizing for different account sizes. Smaller accounts trade 1 contract; larger accounts can scale up using the included sizing rules.
